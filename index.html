KNOW YOUR MATERIALS

Field guide

The forge's rules

Each ID consistently generates two to four types. Combining components combines their types. A counter to any target type earns a normal win; advanced expedition stages require a counter to every target type.

New target/recipe combinations earn research XP. Repeating the same research does not award more base XP. Expedition stages and daily wins offer separate bonuses. Your progression is stored on this device; export a backup to move it.
